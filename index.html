Scooby cluster

The track-day display pod for a 1993 Impreza wagon with an EJ207. Track, Road and PDM are the app's screens, running a simulated lap. The rest are concepts that aren't built yet: night map, new-track map, night vision, a race grid, and a round tach that can bring in a map.

A pod, not a replacement

It clips onto the dash for track days and comes off afterwards. The factory cluster stays in the car.

What runs it

A Raspberry Pi 5 drives a 1920 × 720 panel with a Qt app. It listens to the Emtron KV8 engine computer and the Hardwire PDM-28 power module over CAN.

Warnings that stay put

A low oil pressure or a tripped output latches a red card until the driver acknowledges it. If the data stops, every reading turns to dashes instead of freezing.

Where it stands

The app runs on a desktop with this simulated lap. It hasn't been in the car yet.

Ideas next

A dark map for night and canyon roads, a track map drawn from the first lap, and a thermal night-vision view are recorded as an idea, with nothing decided. The race grid and the round tach with its map are design directions to grow into.
