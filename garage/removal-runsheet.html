Never reinstall the crossmember bolts with an impact. Fit each socket before you lean on it: most sizes are M or L.

Scope and starting state

This runsheet takes the 5-speed out of the car and stops there. The 6-speed and R180 install gets its own runsheet.

Starting state, read from your photos (M, photo read):

  • Engine out. The 5-speed still sits in the tunnel on its rear crossmember.
  • Rear diff out (H). Both rear axles hang free, and the diff mount eyes in the rear crossmember are empty.
  • Driveshaft probably out (M). With the diff gone, a driveshaft still in the car would droop at the back, and nothing droops in the photos. Step 4 covers it if it is still in.

The steps follow the 2002 Impreza service manual (its transmission removal has the same under-car steps) and a GC8 owner's write-up. Confidence tags: H high, M medium, L low.

Procedure

Eight steps, one gate, and one rear diff contingency. Do not take the crossmember off until Gate A is ticked.

  • If the center pipe still hangs from the transmission, unbolt it and lower it. The manual cautions against letting a pipe drop (M)
  • Tools: sockets, extensions, penetrating oil
  • Photograph first, then unplug the transmission harness connectors and the ground terminal (H, manual)
  • Speedometer: unscrew the speedometer cable from the transmission, passenger side. The 1993 Impreza uses a cable (H, parts catalog)
  • Tape and label each plug
  • Tools: hands for the plugs; pliers or fingers on the speedometer cable's knurled collar. Ground terminal size not recorded, try 12 mm (L). No impact
  • Unhook the cable from the release lever, then free it from its bracket on the transmission (M, clip style not checked)
  • Tools: pliers, plus a wrench for the locknut (size not recorded, L). No impact
  • With the rear diff out, the center bearing is what still holds it: 2 x 14 mm bolts on a GC8 (M)
  • If the rear diff is still in: remove the diff mount front cover, mark both flanges, then remove the 4 shaft-to-diff bolts, leaving one in until the center bearing is off (H, 1995 Impreza manual). Size: one Subaru owner reports 12 mm, model not given (L). Hand tools, wrench on the nut
  • Support the shaft, unbolt the bearing, then slide the front yoke rearward out of the transmission
  • Drain pan under the extension housing
  • Tools: 14 mm socket (M). Crack each bolt loose by hand, then the impact can run it out. They thread into the body (M)
  • Remove the stay from the transmission bracket, and the rod from the joint (M, 2002 manual)
  • Unbolt the cushion rubber from the body: 2 x 12 mm on a GC8 (M)
  • Remove the joint and drive out its spring pin
  • Tools: 12 mm for the cushion rubber (M). Stay and rod bolts not recorded, start with 12 then 14 mm (L). Pin punch and hammer. Hand tools only: a 1/2 in impact can snap these small bolts
  • Remove the sway bar link from the control arm, then disconnect the control arm from the knuckle at the ball joint (H, manual)
  • Pull the control arm down with the long pry bar and swing the knuckle out
  • Drive the spring pin out through the chamfered side of its hole, then slide the axle off the stub (M)
  • Hang each axle with wire so its joints do not hang at full angle (Proposed)
  • The pins are single use. The manual says always use a new spring pin on reassembly (H)
  • Tools: sway bar link nut not recorded, try 14 then 17 mm (L). The impact is fine here and helps when the stud spins
  • Ball joint pinch bolt: 14 mm reported on a 2011 Impreza (L). No impact: owners report snapped pinch bolts. Soak it and work it back and forth by hand
  • Also: long pry bar, Company23 511 or a long pin punch, wire
  • Walk around the case: no cable, harness clip, hose or linkage still attached
  • Floor jack under the case and strapped to it, and the front held by the hoist or the strap from above, both just taking weight. Nobody under the case from here on
  • Remove the mount-to-crossmember nuts: 2 x 14 mm on a GC8 (M)
  • Remove the crossmember-to-body bolts: 4 x 14 mm and 2 x 17 mm on a GC8 (M)
  • Break each bolt loose by hand. If one turns but does not back out, stop: the welded nut may have broken loose
  • Lower the crossmember away
  • Tools: 14 mm for the mount nuts (M, two sources). Impact is fine
  • Body bolts, 4 x 14 mm and 2 x 17 mm (M; an STI write-up also puts the outer bolts at 17 mm): crack each loose with the breaker bar first, then use the impact only once the bolt backs out cleanly. Never reinstall them with an impact (factory manual, via the STI write-up)
  • Lower both supports together, slowly, watching for snags
  • Then separate the transmission from the rear cushion rubber (the mount) (H, manual)
  • Lift-out alternative (Proposed): chain the case to the engine hoist before step 7 and lift it out the top instead
  • Tools: mount-to-transmission fastener size not recorded (L). Impact is fine once the case sits on the jack
  • Rear exhaust pipe and muffler off, if still on, and the driveshaft out per step 4 (H, manual)
  • Remove the parking brake cable clamps and bracket near the diff (H, manual)
  • Pop each rear axle DOJ out of the diff and wire the axle to the rear crossmember. The manual uses tool ST 28099PA100 (H); a pry bar on the joint, never on the seal, is the home method (Proposed)
  • Drain the gear oil first so it cannot spill when the diff tips (Proposed). Plug type not recorded
  • Remove the lower differential bracket (H, manual)
  • Transmission jack under the diff and strapped to it. Nobody under the diff from here on
  • Remove the self-locking nuts on the two long rear-facing diff-to-crossmember bolts: 17 mm (M, one owner source for Impreza and Forester)
  • Front member to body: loosen bolt A first, remove bolts B, then bolt A, with a helper holding the member so it cannot drop (H, manual). Size not recorded (L)
  • Lower the jack slowly while moving the diff forward. The front member comes out with it (H, manual)
  • Tools: 17 mm for the crossmember bolts, crack loose by hand, then impact is fine; front member bolts not recorded, try 14 then 17 mm (L); pry bar; drain pan; wire
  • On reinstall: new self-locking nuts (Proposed), new circlip and side oil seal on each axle (H, manual), and keep the front member's upper and lower stoppers in their original order (H, manual)

Post-op

Keep the crossmember and its bolts for the 6-speed, retire the 5-speed mount, and fix any welded nut before install day.
